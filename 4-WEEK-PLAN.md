## 8 Feature Ideas (Beyond Current Site)

| # | Feature | Description | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chaos Rank** | Cookie-based badge system (Rookie Gremlin → Supreme Troublemaker). No account needed; email to save. | Repeat visits + email list |
| 2 | **Prank Generator** | Input target + occasion + risk level → custom prank plan + shareable URL | Viral share + SEO |
| 3 | **Report Card Meme Maker** | Fake school report with silly grades, branded download image | Viral UGC |
| 4 | **Weekly Chaos Challenge** | New challenge every Monday, submissions to Wall of Chaos, winners get discount | Return visits |
| 5 | **Design Your Tee Preview** | Pick slogan + colour → share link to parent to buy. Conversion tool disguised as a toy | Direct conversion |
| 6 | **Vibe Selector / Playlist** | Pick chaos mood → Spotify playlist embed + matching product rec | Dwell time + product discovery |
| 7 | **Chaos vs. Rules Vote** | Weekly absurd debate with live chaos-o-meter results | Engagement + return visits |
| 8 | **Parent Zone** | `/for-parents` page behind "This is extremely boring, adults only" toggle. Gift guides, sizing, brand story | Parent SEO without polluting kid voice |

## Priority Summary

The single highest-ROI execution sequence:

1. **Blog + gift guide articles** (Week 1-2) — content gap left open by competitors
2. **Prank Generator** (Week 2) — viral share mechanic, ranks for "pranks for kids", low build cost
3. **Weekly Challenge** (Week 3) — drives return visits and Wall of Chaos volume without new inventory
4. **Design Your Tee tool** (Week 4) — highest direct conversion impact; kids do the product selection for parents
